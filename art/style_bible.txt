STYLE: dark-fantasy dungeon pixel art matching Image 1, 16x16-pixel tile scale, a hero about 26 pixels tall. Every pixel is a hard square of one flat colour on one even grid; no anti-aliasing, no blur, no gradients, no noise.
Shading: 3-4 flat tone steps per material, hard terminator, hue-shifted ramps: warm shadows go dark red-brown (#36120f, #231010), stone shadows go cold blue-grey (#252534, #141420); highlights warm cream (#eed68a), never pure white.
Palette: low-key and muted like Image 1: charcoal and slate stone (#3e3234, #6b5c56), rust and leather browns (#552f25, #733a21, #934218), deep reds (#73140d, #4f150c), bone (#c8b89a), small accents of ember orange (#f03604, #c25d1a), toxic green (#52c04f) and arcane blue (#64bdea).
Material accents: gold uses #9f5116 / #e2881e / #f9ce4a (the concept's coin ramp, as in chest_open_gold); dark green bronze and moss use #1a4135; spider chitin and silk use #2a202f / #51384e / #5f3d60 / #a47f84 / #895e54 / #1d131f; still water #0f2a2e. Keep blue, green and red gemstones distinct; gold should read yellow rather than copper.
Line: a 1-pixel dark outline (#231010 or #060506) around characters, items and props; stone and floor read through dark mortar lines instead of outlines.
Detail: chunky readable silhouettes, detail only on faces, weapons and focal props; big quiet stone areas.
Light: even neutral studio light from the top-left, soft and moderately bright so the art can be relit in the game; no light sources lit, flames drawn unlit, no glow, no light rays, no cast shadows.
View: top-down 3/4 dungeon-crawler view (floors seen from above, walls, props and characters show their front faces).
No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no watermark, no UI frames.

Biome reference policy: ref.png remains the authority for camera, scale, character designs, outlines and overall rendering. The material and local-light references, their approval status and their allowed scope are recorded in study/refs/README.md. A biome concept does not override this asset's neutral studio lighting: paint flames unlit and omit baked glow and shadows even when a concept shows them lit. Never copy a concept's taller walls, smaller hero, recoloured monsters or whole-frame water/lava coverage. Natural cave materials belong to future cave biomes, not the existing masonry floors. Approve a biome reference before generating its assets.
Approved biome materials (town: see the Town block below): ref_dungeon — dark slate masonry, small cobblestones and warm local light; ref_crypt — cold stone, bone, sarcophagi, moss and green local light with warm candle areas; ref_lava — liquid orange lava, dark crust, hot fissures, channels, stone banks and crossings; ref_arcane — cold stone, blue light sources and crystals with warm side rooms. Local-light colours guide runtime lighting, not baked illumination in assets.
Approved future cave references: ref_cave_water is the primary appearance reference for quiet blue-grey rock, dark teal water, gravel, moss, luminous mushrooms and crystals, waterfalls and a warm explorer camp; ref_cave_water_camera_v2 supplements it for broad connected floor and calm water only. ref_cave_lava supplies basalt, lava crust, lavafalls and natural stone arches. Keep all cave banks and walls low for the gameplay camera; do not copy the concepts' high cliffs or pillars.
Magma caves (R-36, ref_cave_lava, approved second version): the ground is plateaus of ash and basalt grit over lava and a dark chasm, not halls cut in rock. Rock is neutral near-black basalt (#0e0d0e, #1c1a1b, #2e2b2b, #4a4544), never brown, never red: the colour comes from the lava, not from the stone. Basalt breaks into flat-topped hexagonal and angular columns with sharp vertical facets and no masonry; plateau edges show low column faces (10-15 px in game), never the concept's tall pillars. Floor: grey-black ash and basalt crumbs over dark stone, faint dark-red veins (#4f150c, #73140d) in the cracks, painted unlit. Shore: cooled lava crust, black glassy plates with dull rust seams, painted unlit; the hot rim and glowing cracks are runtime light and shaders, not baked art. Lava itself is a shader, never a painted asset.
Town (R-17, ref_town, approved version 01f; crops in study/refcrops/town_*.png): the start town is the night courtyard of a ruined fortress above the crypt entrance, a safe hub where nature is taking the stone back. Buildings are low broken walls of stone blocks around open-roofed rooms, niches and stalls under flat cloth awnings, never houses with roofs or facades.
Town materials: stone is slate blocks with a faint violet-brown tint (#2a2128, #372b34, #4b3943, #5f464f, top edge #81676f) and dark mortar, moss and ivy on the tops; the ground is dark umber earth (#1c1712, #2a1d17, #432e22) with tufts of olive grass and moss (#302b09, #473e0b, #675a0d), never yellow-green or bright green, small white and violet wildflowers as rare accents; paths and the square are worn cobble paving of the same slate; wood is dark weathered timber in rust and leather browns (#281510, #3f261c, #743f1d, edge #a86d32); cloth is deep red (#73140d, #4f150c) for the merchant's awning and banners, dull canvas elsewhere; iron is dark grey with rust. Keep the service designs of the crops: merchant stall with a red awning, barrels and crates; forge as a round stone hearth with an anvil; enchanter lectern with open book on a rune circle beside shelves of flasks; Altar of Souls exactly as the game's shrine, with the iron-bound storage chest beside it; crypt stairs as a dark opening in the floor between two braziers; portal as a ring of low standing stones round an engraved stone disc; notice board on two posts; mine adit with a timber frame and lantern, ore cart, rails on wooden sleepers.
Town light in assets: the concept is lit, the assets are not. Campfire, braziers, torches, lanterns, candles and forge coals are painted unlit (dark wood, cold grey coals, dark wicks); runes and the altar are dark grey grooves with no blue glow; no warm pools, no light spill on the floor or walls. The fire pools, forge glow and the blue light of the enchanter and altar are runtime lights.
Town height and layout: in the game every wall, building, stall, adit, standing stone and tree is one to two floor tiles tall (32-64 game pixels; the mine adit and the campfire tree at most 64-70), never the concept's 105-150 px; walls show only a low front face and never hide the floor or the hero; tree canopies never cover a path or a service. Rails run only along the lower wall to the unloading yard with its ore bin, never across the square or the paths to services. Townsfolk are at hero scale in muted greys, browns and dull greens.
