Use case: a floor decal for the start town of a top-down dungeon game: the enchanter's rune circle carved into the floor under his lectern. Image 1 is the style reference for pixel size, line and stone rendering. Image 2 is the approved town concept's enchanter crop: copy the look of its rune ring, NOT its blue light.
Subject: seen straight from above, a THIN carved ring of runes: two thin concentric grooves (each 1 art pixel wide) about 3 art pixels apart, with small angular rune glyphs (not letters of any real alphabet) cut between them all the way round, and four tiny notches at the quarter points. The whole middle of the ring is EMPTY and transparent; outside the ring is transparent too: only the ring itself is drawn.
Colours: dark grey-violet carved stone grooves (#1c161c, #2a2128, #4b3943), a faint lighter edge on the lower lip of each groove (#5f464f); no blue, no glow.
Composition: one perfect circle centred and filling about 90% of the square canvas; the ring band is about 8% of the diameter wide. Exactly round (the game squashes it into an ellipse).
Constraints: painted UNLIT: no glow, no light; no disc fill, no floor texture inside or outside the ring, no lectern, no props, no text.
