Biome «Dungeon» (floors 1-2): the old castle dungeon of Image 1's centre. Grey-brown cobblestone floors, warm orange wall torches and a standing brazier, candles. Rooms: a guard room with weapon racks and a barrel stack, a prison block with iron cages and a cell door, a small library corner with a bookshelf and a stone statue. One room has a shallow water feature: a narrow stream entering from an iron grate in the wall, running across the floor in a stone channel crossed by a slab bridge, ending in a drain; a small wall fountain (water spouting from a lion-head mouth into a stone basin). Monsters: skeletons with swords and shields, a green goblin, a big dark-blue spider. Cobwebs in corners, scattered bones, a few gold coins, a closed wooden chest.
