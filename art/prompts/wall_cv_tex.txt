Use case: a seamless tileable material for the low front face of natural cave rock in a top-down 3/4 dungeon game (Image 1 is the style reference for pixel size, line and shading; Image 2 is the approved cave material reference: take only its rock material and palette, not its tall cliffs).
Subject: the front face of natural cave rock seen straight-on: rough fractured blue-grey rock with irregular rounded bulges and lumps, uneven dark cracks running mostly diagonally and vertically, a few chipped ledges catching the top-left light, cold slate blue-grey (#252534, #3a3d4c, #565a68) with a hint of brown. Big quiet rock areas, chunky readable forms like the rock faces in Image 2.
Composition: the whole canvas is rock face, edge to edge. It must tile seamlessly in both directions. The canvas is exactly 128x128 art pixels, every art pixel drawn as an 8x8 block of one colour.
Constraints: flat even lighting, no vignette, no gradient, no bricks, no masonry, no mortar, no horizontal rows, no stalactites, no moss, no water, no torches, no objects.
