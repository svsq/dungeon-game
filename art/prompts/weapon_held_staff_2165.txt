Use case: the mage's staff held in his hand in a dark-fantasy pixel-art dungeon RPG; it stands taller than the mage, its crystal above his head, drawn small in the game and three times larger in the main menu against warm brown stone masonry, where it must not merge with it. Image 3 is the old staff icon: only its composition, angle and crop, not its carved gold.
Subject: one very long slender wizard's staff lying diagonally, its foot at the bottom left corner, its head at the top right corner, as in Image 3. The shaft is long and thin, crooked dark wood, a deep cool brown close to black-brown with a lighter grey-brown highlight line along one side, two or three small knots and a slight twist, a strong dark outline along both sides. The head is compact, only about a seventh of the staff's length: thin twisted branches curl around a faceted glowing blue crystal like a cage. No gold, no metal fittings, no carving.
Constraints: exactly one weapon, complete and uncut, with a wide empty margin, on a flat pure magenta (#ff00ff) background; no shadow on the background, no glow, no frame, no hand, no text; crisp pixel art with hard square pixels, a dark 1-pixel outline all round, no anti-aliasing and no gradients.
