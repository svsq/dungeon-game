Use case: a seamless tileable material for the low front face of basalt rock in a top-down 3/4 dungeon game: plateau edges over lava and the cave's back wall (Image 1 is the style reference for pixel size, line and shading; Image 2 is the approved magma cave reference: take only its basalt column faces and palette, not their height and not their lava glow).
Subject: the front faces of basalt columns seen straight-on: tightly packed vertical prismatic columns 6 to 14 art pixels wide with sharp vertical facets, each column showing a lit left facet and a darker right facet, irregular broken horizontal joints across a few columns at different heights, thin near-black gaps between columns. Neutral near-black and dark charcoal grey (#0e0d0e, #1c1a1b, #2e2b2b, #4a4544, highlights #625c5a), never brown, never red.
Composition: the whole canvas is column faces, edge to edge. It must tile seamlessly in both directions. The canvas is exactly 128x128 art pixels, every art pixel an 8x8 block of one colour.
Constraints: flat even lighting, no vignette, no gradient, no glow, no lava, no fire, no bricks, no masonry, no mortar rows, no moss, no water, no objects.
