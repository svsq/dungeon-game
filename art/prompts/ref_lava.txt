Biome «Fire Depths» (floors 5-6): the lava area of Image 1's left third, enlarged into a whole level. Wide rivers of molten lava (3-5 tiles wide) flow through and between the rooms: dark cooled crust plates drifting on bright orange-yellow molten lava, glowing cracks, white-hot spots at the edges, stepped brick banks; one hall is mostly a lava lake with stone islands and pillars standing in it. Every lava river is crossed — by an iron chain bridge with wooden planks, by a stone causeway, or by a path of flat stepping slabs — so every room stays reachable. Scorched dark-red stone floors with soot and rubble, iron cages, weapon racks, a stone statue. Monsters: a big red horned demon, a green goblin, skeletons, a hooded cultist. The hero stands on a stone floor near a bridge. The lava is the main light: orange glow on the walls and floor around it.
