Use case: a seamless tileable paving material for the square and the paths of the start town of a top-down 3/4 dungeon game (Image 1 is the style reference for pixel size, line and shading; Image 2 is the approved town concept: take only its worn paving, its stone size and its palette, never its warm firelight).
Subject: old worn paving seen straight from above, laid like the concept's square: small flat rectangular slate setts about 6 by 4 art pixels each (twice as wide as tall at most), in loose staggered rows, thin dark earth joints of 1 art pixel, many setts chipped, a few sunk or missing with dark earth and a tuft of olive moss in the gap. Flat tops, not rounded cobbles. Slate with a faint violet-brown tint (#2a2128, #372b34, #4b3943, #5f464f, a 1-pixel lighter top edge on some setts #81676f), joints #1c1712.
Composition: the whole canvas is paving, edge to edge. It must tile seamlessly in both directions. The canvas is exactly 128x128 art pixels, every art pixel drawn as an 8x8 block of one colour.
Constraints: flat even lighting, no vignette, no gradient, no warm light, no objects, no walls, no large slabs, no round cobbles.
