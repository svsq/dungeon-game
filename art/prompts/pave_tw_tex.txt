Use case: a seamless tileable cobble road material for the start town of a top-down 3/4 dungeon game. Image 1 is the style reference. Image 2 is the approved town concept's paving crop: copy its stones and their warm contrast, NOT its light pools.
Subject: seen straight from above, SMALL ANGULAR hand-cut paving stones of varied size, 8 to 14 art pixels across, with flat faces and straight broken edges, irregular four- and five-sided shapes, never round pebbles, never long bricks, no straight joint lines across the tile. Stones are clearly LIGHTER than bare earth and warm: worn brown-grey faces (#6b5344, #7f6450, #927561), small top-edge highlights (#ab8c70), some stones darker (#54423a), very dark earth in the thin gaps (#120d0a), a few sunken or missing stones. Strong contrast between the pale stone faces and the near-black gaps.
Composition: the whole canvas is paving, edge to edge, tiling seamlessly in both directions, top edge continuing into bottom edge and left into right with no visible seam, 128x128 art pixels, each art pixel an 8x8 block of one colour. Constraints: flat even light, no gradient, no moss carpets, no glow, no objects, no text.
