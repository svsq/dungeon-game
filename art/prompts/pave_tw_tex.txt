Use case: a seamless tileable cobble road material for the start town of a top-down 3/4 dungeon game. Image 1 is the style reference. Image 2 is the approved town concept's paving crop: copy its stones, NOT its warm light.
Subject: seen straight from above, IRREGULAR rounded-square cobbles of varied size in no running courses, never long bricks, no straight joint lines across the tile; worn dull grey-brown stone (#3a3030, #4a3d39, #5a4a44, small highlights #6f5c52), dark earth in the gaps (#17110e), a few sunken or missing stones.
Composition: the whole canvas is paving, edge to edge, tiling seamlessly in both directions, 144x144 art pixels, each art pixel a 4x4 block. Constraints: flat even light, no gradient, no moss carpets, no glow, no objects, no text.
