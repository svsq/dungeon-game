Use case: a seamless tileable cobble road material for the start town of a top-down 3/4 dungeon game. Image 1 is the style reference. Image 2 is the approved town concept's paving crop: copy its stones, NOT its warm light.
Subject: seen straight from above, SMALL ANGULAR hand-cut paving stones of varied size, 8 to 14 art pixels across, with flat faces and straight broken edges, irregular four- and five-sided shapes, never round pebbles, never long bricks, no straight joint lines across the tile. Stones are clearly LIGHTER than bare earth: dull grey-brown (#5a4c46, #6b5a52, #7c6a5e, small highlights #94806f), dark earth in the thin gaps (#1a1410), a few sunken or missing stones.
Composition: the whole canvas is paving, edge to edge, tiling seamlessly in both directions, 128x128 art pixels, each art pixel an 8x8 block of one colour. Constraints: flat even light, no gradient, no moss carpets, no glow, no objects, no text.
