Use case: a seamless tileable paving material for the start town of a top-down 3/4 dungeon game. Image 1 is the style reference. Image 2 is the approved town concept's paving crop: copy its coursed rows and its warm contrast, NOT its light pools.
Subject: seen straight from above, small roughly rectangular dressed paving stones laid in horizontal coursed rows, every row offset from the next like running bond. Each stone is 13 to 18 art pixels wide and 8 to 11 art pixels tall, with slightly worn corners and small differences in size, never round pebbles, never polygons, never long bricks. Stone faces are warm and clearly lighter than earth: worn brown-grey (#6b5344, #7f6450, #927561), a one-pixel top-edge highlight (#ab8c70), about one stone in six darker (#54423a), very dark earth in the thin even one-pixel joints (#120d0a), one or two chipped stones.
Composition: the whole canvas is paving, edge to edge, tiling seamlessly in both directions, top edge continuing into bottom edge and left into right, the row pattern unbroken across the seam, 128x128 art pixels, each art pixel an 8x8 block of one colour.
Constraints: flat even light, no gradient, no moss carpets, no glow, no objects, no text.
