Use case: a seamless tileable ground material for the start town of a top-down 3/4 dungeon game, a ruined fortress courtyard taken back by nature (Image 1 is the style reference for pixel size, line and shading; Image 2 is the approved town concept: take only its ground material and palette, never its warm firelight).
Subject: dark umber earth seen straight from above, a cool dark brown, never red or rust (#1c1712, #2a1d17, #3a2a1f, #432e22), packed and slightly uneven, with many tufts and soft patches of olive grass and moss (#302b09, #473e0b, #675a0d) covering a full quarter of the surface in irregular clumps of different sizes, a few tiny pebbles and one or two lone slate cobbles half sunk in the earth. No yellow-green, no bright green.
Composition: the whole canvas is ground, edge to edge. It must tile seamlessly in both directions. The canvas is exactly 128x128 art pixels, every art pixel drawn as an 8x8 block of one colour.
Constraints: flat even lighting, no vignette, no gradient across the image, no warm light pools, no flowers, no objects, no paths, no walls.
