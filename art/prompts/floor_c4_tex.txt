Use case: a seamless tileable floor material for a top-down 3/4 dungeon game. Image 1 is the style reference: copy the stone floor of its green crypt area at the top right (not the walls).
Subject: an old crypt flagstone floor seen straight from above: big worn stone slabs of DIFFERENT sizes laid in an irregular pattern (some large squares, some long rectangles, some small filler stones, a few broken slabs split by cracks), chipped corners, uneven dark mortar with small patches of moss and grime in the joints; cold desaturated green-grey stone with slight variation in tone from slab to slab. Not a regular grid, not bathroom tiles.
Composition: the whole canvas is floor, edge to edge, tiling seamlessly in both directions. About 7 large slabs across the width. The canvas is 160x160 art pixels, every art pixel drawn as a block of one colour.
Constraints: flat even lighting, no vignette, no gradient across the image, no objects, no bones, no walls.
