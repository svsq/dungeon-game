Town variant 1: the courtyard of a ruined fortress built over the crypt entrance, at dusk. Layout: a wide square courtyard of worn grey flagstones enclosed by low broken curtain walls (one to two tiles high, gaps and fallen blocks), a collapsed gatehouse at the bottom edge opening into black void. In the centre a stone fire pit with a big campfire and log benches. Along the top wall: the crypt stairs going down in a ruined keep doorway frame (low, broken), flanked by two braziers. Left side: the merchant under a faded red cloth awning against the wall, a counter of planks on barrels. Right side: the forge built into a ruined tower stump (only its low round base remains) with an anvil, and next to it the enchanter in a roofless chapel nook with a lectern and blue runes on the floor. Bottom right: the Altar of Souls on a small raised dais with the storage chest beside it. Bottom left: a ring of low standing stones for the portal among grass tufts. Mood: cold blue-grey dusk light faintly on the flagstones, the warm fire and forge as the main light pools, moss and ivy on the broken walls. Townsfolk: a woman sweeping, a man chopping wood near a woodpile, a guard leaning on a spear by the stairs, an old man sleeping by the fire.
