Use case: a sheet of flat floor decals for a top-down 3/4 dungeon game, laid over the stone floor to break up repetition. Image 1 is the style reference: copy the floor dirt, blood, cracks and debris of its rooms.
Subject: 16 separate flat floor decals seen from directly above, in a 4x4 grid with wide empty gaps: a branching crack in stone; a dark grime stain; a patch of green moss between stones; a scatter of small rubble and pebbles; a pool of dried dark blood; a smear of fresh blood; a few scattered gold coins; a broken rusty sword lying flat; a scattered rib cage and bones; a small puddle of dark water; a patch of loose straw; scorched black soot mark; a broken clay pot in shards; a scatter of dead leaves and dust; a cobweb in a corner shape; a cracked broken floor slab.
Scale: each decal about 1 to 2 floor tiles across, the hero would be 2.6 tiles tall.
Constraints: every decal flat on the floor (no height, no shadow), separate, nothing touching, no glow, no text.
