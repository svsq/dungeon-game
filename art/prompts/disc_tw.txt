Use case: one prop for a top-down 3/4 dungeon game, the stone floor disc inside a portal ring of standing stones. Image 1 is the style reference. Image 2 is the approved town concept's portal crop: match its engraved disc, NOT its size and NOT its light.
Subject: one round flat disc of dark slate (#372b34, #4b3943, #5f464f, edge #81676f) seen from above in the squashed 3/4 view, about 90 art pixels wide and 58 tall, carved with deep, wide grooves two art pixels thick and near black (#141012): two concentric rings near the rim, four short radial ticks, and one clear simple star-like sigil in the centre. The disc is made of a few big wedge-shaped stones with thin joints, chipped on the rim.
Constraints: centred, complete, on a transparent background. Painted UNLIT: no glow, no blue light, no shadow on the ground, no text, no letters.
