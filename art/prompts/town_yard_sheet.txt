Image 2 shows crops of the approved town concept (the campfire with its benches, the log pile, the herb bed, the mine with its ore cart and pick rack): match their designs and palette, NOT their size and NOT their warm light. Subject: 8 separate props in the 3/4 view of Image 1, in a 4x2 grid with wide empty gaps, each on one shared ground line per row:
row 1: a low wooden bench of a split log on two stubby legs; a stack of cut logs, round ends toward the viewer, with two loose planks; a raised wooden herb bed of planks filled with dark earth, leafy green herbs and a few purple lavender spikes; a small iron-wheeled wooden mine cart heaped with grey-blue ore, seen from its side at 3/4;
row 2: a wooden rack with two pick-axes and a shovel leaning in it; a pile of three rough-cut pale grey quarry stone blocks; a campfire ring of rounded stones around a few crossed logs and cold grey ash, UNLIT; a small heap of grey-blue ore chunks on the ground.

Constraints: every prop complete and separate, nothing touching. Painted UNLIT: no flames, no embers, no glow, no light, no smoke, no shadows on the ground, no floor, no text.
