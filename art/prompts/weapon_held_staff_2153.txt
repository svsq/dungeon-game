Subject: one tall wizard's staff lying diagonally, its foot at the bottom left, its head at the top right, as in Image 3. The shaft is crooked dark wood, a deep cool brown close to black-brown with a few lighter grey-brown highlights, gnarled with two or three knots and a slight twist, a strong dark outline along both sides. At the top, thin twisted branches curl around a faceted glowing blue crystal like a cage. No gold, no metal fittings, no carving.
Constraints: exactly one weapon, complete and uncut, with a wide empty margin, on a flat pure magenta (#ff00ff) background; no shadow on the background, no glow, no frame, no hand, no text; crisp pixel art with hard square pixels, a dark 1-pixel outline all round, no anti-aliasing and no gradients.
