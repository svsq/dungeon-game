Use case: the smithy of the start town of a top-down 3/4 dungeon game. Image 1 is the style reference. Image 2 is the approved town concept's forge crop: copy that layout closely: a low HORSESHOE WALL of stacked slate blocks open toward the viewer, and set in its back a low round-arched brick hearth full of burning coals, tools hanging on the wall beside it. It is an open stone enclosure, never a free-standing dome, oven or bread oven, no wooden door. NOT the warm light of Image 2.
Materials: slate #2a2128, #372b34, #4b3943, #5f464f, top edge #81676f, neutral grey-violet bricks too, never pink or red; coals #f03604, #c25d1a, #f9ce4a among black; iron dark grey.
View and scale: 3/4 view; 44 art pixels wide, 30 tall (hero 26, barrel 14). Composition: one prop, centred, wide empty margin, transparent background. Constraints: no anvil, no figures, no ground, no shadow, no glow halo, no text.
