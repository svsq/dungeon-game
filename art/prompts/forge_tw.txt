View and scale: the 3/4 view of Image 1, front face visible, top of the dome seen slightly from above; the forge is 18 art pixels tall and 22 wide (a barrel is 14, the hero 26).
Composition: one prop centred, complete, wide empty margin, transparent background.
Constraints: painted UNLIT: cold coals, no fire, no glow, no ember light, no light spill; no ground, no shadow, no anvil, no other props, no text.
