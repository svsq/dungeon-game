Use case: a seamless tileable floor material for a top-down 3/4 dungeon game. Image 1 is the style reference: copy the stone floor of its blue arcane hall at the right (not the walls, no runes).
Subject: an old arcane hall floor seen straight from above: big worn dark slate slabs of DIFFERENT sizes laid in an irregular pattern (some large squares, some long rectangles, some small filler stones, a few cracked slabs), chipped corners, thin dark mortar, a little dust in the joints; cold blue-grey slate with slight variation in tone from slab to slab, a few slabs slightly smoother and darker. Not a regular grid, not bathroom tiles.
Composition: the whole canvas is floor, edge to edge, tiling seamlessly in both directions. About 7 large slabs across the width. The canvas is 160x160 art pixels, every art pixel drawn as a block of one colour.
Constraints: flat even lighting, no vignette, no gradient across the image, no glow, no runes, no symbols, no objects, no walls.
