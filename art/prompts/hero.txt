Subject: a young but rugged western-fantasy warrior: a squarish face with a strong jaw, small narrow eyes under heavy dark brows, messy short brown hair (not spiky, not tall); steel shoulder plates and breastplate over a dark tunic, a red cape, brown leather gloves and boots; standing in the 3/4 front-right view of Image 4 (body turned toward the lower right of the screen).
Proportions: realistic heroic proportions: the head including the hair is 1/4.5 of the full height (much smaller than Image 4), broad shoulders about twice the head width, a solid torso, legs about half the height. Small dark eyes 1 art pixel tall, no big irises, no eye highlights.
Pose: neutral rig pose: standing straight, feet a little apart directly under the hips with a clear gap between the legs, both arms hanging slightly away from the body with a gap between arms and torso, empty hands (no weapon, no shield), cape hanging straight behind.
Composition: one character centred, full body, filling about 70% of the canvas height, wide empty margin. The character is 26 art pixels tall, every art pixel drawn as a block of one colour.
Constraints: one character only, no weapon, no shield, no shadow on the ground, no floor.
