Use case: a seamless tileable material for the low front face of the ruined walls of the start town in a top-down 3/4 dungeon game (Image 1 is the style reference for pixel size, line and shading; Image 2 is the approved town concept: take only its ruined block walls and palette, not their height and not their firelight).
Subject: the front face of an old dry-stone wall seen straight-on: rough squarish slate blocks (about 10 to 14 art pixels across) in uneven rows, some blocks chipped or missing their corners, dark mortar gaps, small strands of ivy and moss hanging over a few blocks. Slate with a faint violet-brown tint (#2a2128, #372b34, #4b3943, #5f464f, edge glints #81676f), moss and ivy olive (#302b09, #473e0b, #675a0d).
Composition: the whole canvas is wall face, edge to edge. It must tile seamlessly in both directions. The canvas is exactly 128x128 art pixels, every art pixel an 8x8 block of one colour.
Constraints: flat even lighting, no vignette, no gradient, no torches, no doors, no windows, no objects.
