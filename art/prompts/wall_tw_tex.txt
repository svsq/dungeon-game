Use case: a seamless tileable wall-face material for the ruined fortress of the start town of a top-down 3/4 dungeon game. Image 1 is the style reference. Image 2 is the approved town concept's crop of its ruined walls: copy its stone: separate STACKED FLAT ANGULAR SLATE BLOCKS, NOT its warm light.
Subject: seen straight on, courses of grey-violet slate blocks of uneven width, each block with a clearly PALE TOP EDGE two art pixels tall (#81676f, #9a848a) over a mid face (#4b3943, #5f464f) and a dark lower edge (#2a2128), separated by near-black mortar gaps (#141018). Slightly chipped corners. Cool, nearly grey, never pink, red or salmon.
Composition: the whole canvas is wall, edge to edge, tiling seamlessly in both directions, 112x112 art pixels, each art pixel a 4x4 block. Constraints: flat even light, no gradient, no moss, no glow, no objects, no text.
