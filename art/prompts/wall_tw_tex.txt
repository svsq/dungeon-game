Use case: a seamless tileable wall-face material for the ruined fortress of the start town of a top-down 3/4 dungeon game. Image 1 is the style reference. Image 2 is the approved town concept's crop of its ruined walls: copy its stone, NOT its warm light.
Subject: seen straight on, MANY small flat angular slate blocks in irregular courses, each block only 14 to 22 art pixels wide and 8 to 12 art pixels tall, of uneven width so no joint lines up across three courses and no pattern repeats. Each block has a pale top edge one art pixel tall (#81676f, #9a848a) over a mid face (#4b3943, #5f464f) and a dark lower edge (#2a2128). Blocks are separated by thin near-black mortar gaps one art pixel wide (#141018). Some corners chipped, a few blocks darker or lighter than their neighbours. Cool, nearly grey with a slight blue-violet cast, hue 270 to 290, never pink, red or salmon.
Composition: the whole canvas is wall, edge to edge, tiling seamlessly in both directions, 128x128 art pixels, each art pixel an 8x8 block of one colour. Constraints: flat even light, no gradient, no moss, no glow, no objects, no text.
