Use case: the mage's wand held in his hand in a dark-fantasy pixel-art dungeon RPG; drawn small in the game and three times larger in the main menu. It must read at a glance as a short wand, clearly not a staff: the mage also carries a tall crooked staff with a crystal in a cage of branches. Image 1 is the style reference only. Image 2 is the approved design of the mage: the dark wood, the blue crystal, the art's pixel density, palette and shading. Image 3 is the old wand icon: only its composition, angle and crop.
Subject: one short straight wand lying diagonally, its butt at the bottom left, its tip at the top right, as in Image 3. A slim smooth rod of dark polished wood, straight, tapering a little toward the tip, no knots, no branches; the lower third is a grip wrapped in dark leather with a small round dark wood knob at the butt; at the tip a single large faceted blue gem, an elongated crystal point about three times as wide as the rod and a sixth of the wand's length, held by a short dark wooden collar. A strong dark outline, a light highlight line along the rod. No gold, no cage of branches, no orb.
Constraints: exactly one weapon, complete and uncut, with a wide empty margin, on a flat pure magenta (#ff00ff) background; no shadow on the background, no glow, no frame, no hand, no text; crisp pixel art with hard square pixels, a dark 1-pixel outline all round, no anti-aliasing and no gradients.
