Use case: a seamless tileable floor material for the magma caves' plateaus in a top-down 3/4 dungeon game (Image 1 is the style reference for pixel size, line and shading; Image 2 is the approved magma cave reference: take only the walkable ash-and-basalt ground of its plateaus and its palette, not its lava or its light).
Subject: volcanic plateau ground seen straight from above, no masonry: packed grey-black ash and fine basalt grit with scattered small angular basalt crumbs and chips (2 to 8 art pixels), a few larger flat dark stone patches with irregular angular edges (10 to 24 art pixels, never rectangular, never in rows), thin branching cracks between them with a faint dull dark-red tint (#4f150c, #73140d) deep inside a few cracks only. Neutral near-black and charcoal grey (#141314, #232122, #34302f, #4a4544), slightly cooler in the stone patches, never brown, never orange: the warm colour in the game comes from lava light, not from this texture.
Composition: the whole canvas is ground, edge to edge. It must tile seamlessly in both directions. The canvas is exactly 128x128 art pixels, every art pixel drawn as an 8x8 block of one colour.
Constraints: flat even neutral lighting with no vignette, no gradient across the image, no glow, no lava, no fire, no bright orange, no bricks, no slabs in a grid, no mortar lines, no objects, no bones, no props, no walls.
