Use case: the desktop app icon of «The Elder Deep», a dark-fantasy top-down dungeon crawler of skeletons, cultists and demons in the deep under a ruined fortress town. Image 1 is the style reference only (pixel rendering, palette, outline), NOT its layout or its camera. Concept: a creature.
Subject: the skull of a horned demon seen from straight in front, filling the frame: a broad heavy bone skull (#c8b89a, shade #8a7a64, deep shade #4f3a2a, highlight #eed68a) with a cracked brow, two thick horns of dark rust-red (#73140d, #4f150c, highlight #934218) sweeping up and out from the temples into a bold crescent, deep black eye sockets (#060506) each holding one small ember-orange point (#f03604, core #eed68a), a dark nose hole, and a short row of big square teeth. Menacing but simple, a few big shapes.
Pixel size: the whole square image is 48 art pixels across, every art pixel a hard square of one flat colour on one even grid, 1-pixel dark outline (#060506) round the motif, 3-4 flat tones per material, light from the top-left. Big flat shapes only: no part of the motif thinner than 2 art pixels, no fine filigree, no small scattered bits; it must still read as one clear shape when the whole image is shrunk to 16x16 pixels.
Composition: a desktop application icon. Square, one centred motif, front view (an exception to the game camera of Image 1), filling about three quarters of the square with an even empty margin of about 6 art pixels on every side, nothing touching the edges. Full-bleed flat opaque background of dark slate (#141420) across the whole square, no transparency, no vignette, no gradient.
Constraints: one motif, no text, no letters, no numbers, no logo, no frame, no border, no rounded-corner plate, no glow halo, no light rays, no cast shadow, no UI. Original design, not an imitation of any existing game icon.
