Use case: the desktop app icon of «The Elder Deep», a dark-fantasy top-down dungeon crawler where a hero carries the fire of the town square down into the deep under a ruined fortress town. Image 1 is the style reference only (pixel rendering, palette, outline), NOT its layout or its camera. Image 2 is the lantern to reuse (its iron cage, ring handle, red rag and teardrop flame), Image 3 is the stone doorway to reuse (its slate blocks, lintel and steps into black); take their shapes and materials, NOT their layouts, and drop everything of Image 3 that is not named below. Concept: the lantern at the gate.
Subject: a thick round-topped stone arch of a few big slate blocks (#2a2128, #372b34, #4b3943, #5f464f, top edge #81676f) with dark mortar, a big wedge-shaped keystone at its crown; the opening under the arch is a black stairwell, three or four wide steps going down, each darker than the one above, the lower half pure black (#060506). From the keystone, on a short heavy chain of two or three big iron links, hangs an old iron lantern as in Image 2: a heavy square cage of dark rusted iron (#3e3234, #552f25, highlight #6b5c56) with a thick ring handle on top, a stout base and four thick bars, inside it one bold teardrop flame in flat ember pixels (#4f150c rim, #f03604, #c25d1a, core #eed68a), and a short strip of deep-red cloth (#73140d, #4f150c) knotted round the handle. The flame is the brightest thing in the image and the one lit thing, painted as flat colour with no glow halo. The lantern hangs in the upper middle of the black opening, about a third of the image tall, its dark iron silhouetted against the black by the 1-pixel slate-coloured rim light on its left edge. Only the top edge of the first step catches a thin flat warm tone (#c25d1a) under the lantern. Symmetric, grim, warm, inviting.
Pixel size: the whole square image is 48 art pixels across, every art pixel a hard square of one flat colour on one even grid, 1-pixel dark outline (#060506) round the motif, 3-4 flat tones per material, light from the top-left. Big flat shapes only: no part of the motif thinner than 2 art pixels, no fine filigree, no small scattered bits; it must still read as one clear shape when the whole image is shrunk to 16x16 pixels: a bright flame inside a black opening inside a stone frame.
Composition: a desktop application icon. Square, one centred motif, front view (an exception to the game camera of Image 1), filling about three quarters of the square with an even empty margin of about 6 art pixels on every side, nothing touching the edges. Full-bleed flat opaque background of dark slate (#141420) across the whole square, no transparency, no vignette, no gradient.
Constraints: one motif, no text, no letters, no numbers, no logo, no picture frame or border round the square, no rounded-corner plate, no glow halo, no light rays, no cast shadow, no UI, no braziers, no torches, no moss, no ivy, no vines. Original design, not an imitation of any existing game icon.
