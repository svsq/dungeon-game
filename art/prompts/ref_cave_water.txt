Biome «Flooded Caves» — a NATURAL CAVE, not a built dungeon: this overrides the stone-block walls of the STYLE section. No masonry, no brick walls, no tiled floors: irregular organic cave walls of dark wet rock with rounded lumpy edges and stalagmites, still cut out of the pure black void and drawn with the same chunky pixel clusters and the same top-down 3/4 camera as Image 1. Floors: uneven natural stone slabs, gravel and pebbles, patches of dark green moss and pale glowing mushrooms. Lots of water: an underground river winding through the caverns, a wide cave lake with rocks and islands, a small waterfall pouring from a crack in the rock into a pool with foam and spray, shallow flooded passages the hero can wade through, ripples and light reflections on the water. Crossings: a rope bridge of planks over the river, a ford of flat stepping stones, a narrow rock spit. Light: cold blue-green — glowing mushrooms, luminous crystals in the rock, a few warm torches on wooden posts left by explorers, a campfire; deep black between caverns. Monsters: a big dark-blue spider, a green goblin, skeletons; a few bones, an old wooden chest on an island, rotten crates. The hero stands on a gravel bank by the river fighting 2-3 monsters.
CAMERA (most important): exactly the same flat top-down 3/4 camera as Image 1 — the floor is seen almost straight from above and fills most of the frame; cave walls are LOW, drawn like the wall blocks of Image 1: only a short front face (about one tile high) along the edge of each cavern, never tall cliffs; no tall stalagmites or columns standing on the floor, at most a few short rocks; wide open walkable areas of floor where a fight can happen, not narrow ledges; the same muted, low-key palette and contrast as Image 1, saturated colour only in light sources and the water or lava. Water covers at most 35 % of the frame.
