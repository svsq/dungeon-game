One isolated sprite, seen from above at the top-down 3/4 view: a natural basalt arch spanning a lava river, built of the same thick hexagonal basalt columns as the rest of the magma cave. Two massive piers of packed columns stand left and right, each about as wide as two heroes; between them, high over the river, a span of packed column tops arcs across, about four heroes long, its upper surface a crowded mosaic of clover-shaped and hexagonal column tops with 1-pixel pale worn rims (#625c5a) and dark grooves between them; under the span a tall rounded opening as tall as one and a half heroes. The opening is EMPTY: fully transparent background, nothing painted in it (the game draws the lava through it). The inner faces of the piers and the underside of the span are columns' vertical facets, a lit left facet and a darker right facet with sharp vertical edges and a few horizontal joints, darker toward the opening. Neutral near-black basalt (#0e0d0e, #1c1a1b, #2e2b2b, #4a4544), never brown, never red, never orange. The design follows Image 2 (a crop of the approved magma cave concept: the basalt arch over the lava), without any lava, glow, fire or orange light: painted UNLIT with even neutral light. Chunky pixel-art style of Image 1, 1-pixel dark outline (#060506), 3-4 hard tone steps per facet, no gradients, no anti-aliasing, no fine detail. No lava, no floor, no shadow, no text. Transparent background with generous margins, and transparent inside the opening.
