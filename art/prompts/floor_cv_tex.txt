Use case: a seamless tileable floor material for a natural water cave in a top-down 3/4 dungeon game (Image 1 is the style reference for pixel size, line and shading; Image 2 is the approved cave material reference: take only its floor materials and palette).
Subject: natural cave floor seen straight from above, no masonry: broad flat irregular rock plates with rounded worn edges (most 12 to 30 art pixels across, never rectangular, never in rows), packed grey-brown grit and small rounded pebbles filling the gaps between them, a few tiny dark-green moss tufts in the gaps. Muted cool grey-brown stone with a slight blue-grey hint, quiet large areas like the walkable floor in Image 2, but lit neutrally.
Composition: the whole canvas is floor, edge to edge. It must tile seamlessly in both directions. The canvas is exactly 128x128 art pixels, every art pixel drawn as an 8x8 block of one colour.
Constraints: flat even lighting with no vignette, no gradient across the image, no bricks, no slabs in a grid, no mortar lines, no water, no puddles, no objects, no bones, no props, no walls, no glow.
