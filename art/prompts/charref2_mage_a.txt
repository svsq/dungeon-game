Use case: a CHARACTER CONCEPT REFERENCE sheet (not a game asset) for a playable class of a top-down 3/4 dark-fantasy dungeon action game; the art director picks one design from several and the rig parts are redrawn from it later. Image 1 is the style reference only: match its pixel rendering, outline, palette temperature and light, NOT its characters' big heads. Image 2 is the concept's current hero: take only the material quality of his armour and the warm palette; his big head, boyish face, spiky hair and short cartoon body are exactly what this redesign removes. Image 3 is THIS character's approved design from the previous round: redraw the SAME person (same face, age, hair, beard, build, gear, cape, colours and pose) as a new sheet with only the change described under Revision; do not copy its pixels, draw it fresh in the same style.
Direction: grounded, realistic dark fantasy, NOT anime, NOT chibi, NOT cartoon. An ADULT person with real facial features: a defined nose, a heavy brow ridge, cheekbones, a jaw, small narrow realistic eyes (each eye 1-2 art pixels, dark, no irises, no white shine, no highlights), a plain closed mouth line; skin with a little weathering. Healthy warm skin (ramp #e0a878, #c07a52, #8a4e34, shadow #5a2e22), never pale, never grey, never greenish. Realistic proportions: the head including the hair is ONE SEVENTH of the full figure height (seven heads tall), shoulders about two and a half head widths, a long torso, legs about half the height, hands the size of the face. Hair is realistic and lies flat to the head: cropped short, or tied back, or under a hood; no spiky tufts, no anime fringe over the eyes, no big hair volume.
Materials: worn, used and dirty, nothing new or shiny: scuffed leather with darker creases, dull dented steel with rust at the edges, cloth with real folds, mud on the boots and hems. The cloak or cape is heavy wool cloth with a clear attachment (a brooch, clasp or strap at the shoulders) and vertical folds that read as fabric.
Pose: standing at rest, neutral idle, 3/4 front view (body turned a little toward the lower right of the image, face visible), feet a shoulder width apart, arms relaxed a little away from the body so both arms and both hands are fully visible and separate from the torso and the cloak; the weapon is held in ONE hand with a natural grip (fingers closed round the grip), never floating, never across the legs.
Class: the MAGE, a battle mage who casts fire and frost (kits: staff, wand or an ash staff; an orb in the off hand).
Subject: a gaunt scholar-mage about forty: a long narrow face, a long straight nose, deep-set eyes, a neat greying dark beard, dark hair pulled back and tied at the nape. Gear: a long deep-blue wool robe, its hem dirty and frayed, a darker blue hooded mantle over the shoulders with the hood down, a broad brown leather belt with a pouch and a small book on a chain, brown leather boots, cloth wraps on the forearms. A tall gnarled wooden staff held upright in the right hand, his fist round it at chest height and its foot on the ground beside his right foot; a small arcane-blue stone bound by iron wire into the staff's head. In his LEFT hand, held palm-up a little in front of his hip with the fingers cupped under it, a MAGIC ORB about the size of his head (twice as big as an apple-sized ball): a glass-like arcane sphere with visible depth, a bright pale-cyan core (#e8fbff, #9fe6ff) glowing from inside, swirling bands of blue light (#64bdea, #3a7fd0) curling round the core, two or three faint pale rune marks floating inside it, a dark rim at the bottom edge for volume, and a soft pale-blue halo of light (#64bdea at low strength, a ring of 2-3 art pixels of dim blue around it) that faintly lights the cupped fingers and the near sleeve.
Revision: the ONLY change from Image 3 is the orb in the left hand: it is about TWICE as large as before (about the size of his head) and reads unmistakably as a magical sphere (inner glowing core, depth, swirling light, faint runes, a soft light halo), NOT a plain flat blue ball. The scholar, his face, hair, beard, robe, mantle, belt, book, staff and pose stay exactly as in Image 3. The orb and its halo are the only glow in the image.
Composition: one landscape sheet on a flat dark slate background (#1a1a24). LEFT two thirds: the full figure, head to boots, standing on nothing, about 75% of the sheet height, centred in its area, with a wide empty margin. RIGHT third: a close-up of the SAME character's head and shoulders, the face in the same 3/4 view, at about three times the figure's scale, with the same art-pixel size grid (bigger pixels), so the face design is readable. Nothing else.
Pixel art: dark-fantasy pixel art like Image 1, every art pixel a hard square of one flat colour, 1-pixel dark outline (#231010 or #060506), 3-4 flat tones per material, light from the top-left, no glow anywhere except the orb and its small halo (drawn as flat stepped pixel bands, not an airbrush), no gradients, not a painting and not a 3D render. The full figure is drawn about 110 art pixels tall, with a silhouette chunky and clear enough to still read when shrunk to 32 pixels tall: few big shapes, a strong cloak and weapon shape, and only these colour accents: deep blue wool (#2a4a86, #1f2f5c, light #3f6aa8) with a small arcane-blue stone (#64bdea) and dull bronze-gold trim (#9f5116, #e2881e); the orb in the left hand is the brightest thing in the picture.
Constraints: one character only (shown twice: full figure and head close-up), no second person, no text, no letters, no numbers, no labels, no captions, no frame, no border, no UI, no ground shadow, no floor, no background scenery.
