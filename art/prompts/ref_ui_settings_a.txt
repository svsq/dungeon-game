STYLE: this is a MOCK SCREEN of the user interface of the same game as Image 1: a dark-fantasy top-down pixel-art dungeon crawler. One complete 16:9 landscape frame (1920x1080 layout), the whole screen edge to edge, nothing outside it. Match Image 1 exactly in rendering: every pixel a hard square of one flat colour on one even grid, the UI drawn on the same pixel grid as the game art (one art pixel is a 3x3 block of screen pixels), no anti-aliasing, no blur, no smooth gradients, no vector look, no glossy 3D buttons, no drop shadows from a web design; soft transparency is done with a 1-pixel checker dither. Low-key and muted like Image 1, 1-pixel dark outline (#060506 or #231010) on every frame and icon. Any game scene or hero visible behind or inside the screen is painted like Image 1: same camera, same chunky stone walls, same hero (red cape, steel breastplate, sword), lit by warm torches with deep shadow.
GOAL: the interface of a finished, grown-up commercial pixel-art RPG, deliberate and restrained, not a hobby project: strict alignment, a clear type hierarchy, generous consistent margins, one frame language and one accent colour used everywhere, small and calm controls, no oversized buttons, no clip-art, no random decoration, no rainbow of colours. Original design only: do not imitate the screens, panels, fonts or art of any specific existing game.
TEXT RULES: absolutely no game title, no logo, no studio name, no watermark. Labels are short, sparse, plausible Russian words in a crisp retro bitmap pixel font with Cyrillic letters (chunky, one pixel stroke, even spacing, no italic), set small; where a line of body text would be long, draw it as unreadable pixel glyph marks of the same font rather than inventing words. Numbers only where listed. No English words except keys such as Esc, E, J.

DIRECTION A, "iron and stone" (a forged fortress). Every frame is cut from dark slate stone blocks (#252534, #3e3234, #6b5c56 for the lit top edge) with blackened iron bands along the edges, small rivets on the corners and a worn bronze stud at each corner; chamfered corners, thick 3-4 art-pixel borders, heavy and sturdy. Panel interiors are near-black slate (#141420) with a faint stone texture. Buttons and list rows are plain iron plates; the hover/selected state is a thin ember-orange rim (#c25d1a, #f03604) with a tiny flame-shaped marker. Text is bone (#c8b89a), headings warm cream (#eed68a), disabled text cold grey (#6b5c56). The single accent colour is ember orange; money is gold (#e2881e / #f9ce4a); rarity colours only on items. Any parchment in this direction is a sheet nailed to the stone frame with iron nails, torn at the edges.

SCREEN: the settings window, opened over the dimmed main-menu scene (dungeon gate, black vignette). One centred window about 1000x700 px. Header word: Настройки. Three small tabs: Картинка, Звук, Управление (Картинка active). Rows, label left and control right, even 56 px rhythm: Масштаб с ползунком (slider with five tick marks, the knob between the marks, value written like x1,0 near the knob; the range runs from x0,5 to x1,5), Яркость (slider), Качество графики (three-way segmented switch: Высокое, Среднее, Низкое; Среднее active), Язык (switch: Русский, Українська), Полный экран (small checkbox). At the bottom of the window two small buttons: Назад and Применить. A short hint line under the rows in unreadable glyph marks.

Layout: the window is a slab of riveted slate with an iron title bar; sliders are an iron rail with a notched track and a square iron knob, filled part of the track ember-orange; the segmented switch is three adjacent iron plates, the active one with the ember rim; checkbox is a small iron square with an ember tick.
