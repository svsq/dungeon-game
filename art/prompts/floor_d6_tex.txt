Use case: a seamless tileable floor material for a top-down 3/4 dungeon game. Image 1 is the style reference: copy the paving of its torch-lit halls and passages (not the walls).
Subject: a dungeon floor of small square cobblestones seen straight from above, laid in slightly irregular rows: each stone about 5x5 art pixels (some 4x5, 6x6, a few 6x4), every stone separated from all its neighbours by a continuous 1-art-pixel dark mortar line #141420. Grey-brown stone #3e3234 / #6b5c56 with one lighter step: three flat tone steps per stone (a lighter top-left edge, a darker bottom-right edge), a few chipped stones, a few hairline cracks, dark grime specks in some joints. Not big flagstones, not bathroom tiles.
Composition: the canvas is exactly 128x128 art pixels, every art pixel drawn as an 8x8 block of one colour, about 21 stones across the width. The whole canvas is floor, edge to edge, tiling seamlessly in both directions.
Constraints: flat even lighting, no vignette, no gradient across the image, no smooth shading, no objects, no blood, no walls.
