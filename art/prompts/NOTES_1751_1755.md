
## 3. What to check that it ran

- `art/gen/bg_crypt.png` is 1920×1080, opaque; `ui_b_*` have transparent backgrounds with no magenta fringe (`gen.py validate`).
- `live/assets.js` lists the new names; `prep.py` printed a size line for each.
- Look at each at 100 %: no text, letters or runes; no hero, no figures; no flames in the brazier bowls or the sconces (the game draws
  them); the top third of the backdrop dark and empty; the landing in front of the arch (x ≈ 0.47, y 0.66–0.80) plain.
- `ui_b_menu_leaf`: torn and charred all round, no straight edge, warm tan (base about #c19c79), solid inside; the band y 120–180 of
  300 art px plain. `ui_b_strap`: plain stitched strap above, buckle and riveted tab at the foot, straight bottom edge.
- `bg_crypt_dark`: overlay it on `bg_crypt` (difference image); stones must not move. If they do, drop it: the flicker then uses
  additive glows only.
