Use case: one small hand part for the cut-out rig of the warrior player character in a top-down 3/4 dark-fantasy pixel-art dungeon game: his closed fist round a weapon's haft. A script crops each fist, removes the rod above and below it and draws the fist over the real sword hilt, axe haft or hammer haft, turned along it; in the main menu the fist is shown three times larger, so the fingers must read. Image 1 is the style reference only. Image 2 is the approved design of this warrior: his brown leather gloves and worn steel. Image 3 shows his forearm as drawn now, in the brown leather glove (left) and in the steel plate gauntlet (right): copy their materials, colours, outline and pixel density exactly, NOT their open hand shape.
Subject: two separate closed fists side by side with a wide empty gap between them, each gripping a plain straight vertical rod: left, the brown leather glove of Image 3 left (#5a2e22, #733a21, #934218, highlight #c07a52); right, the steel plate gauntlet of Image 3 right (worn steel finger plates #4a4a52, #7a7a84, #b0b0b8 over dark leather). The rod is flat mid grey (#808080, one tone, no shading, no outline), straight, the same width as one finger is tall, running vertically through the middle of the fist and sticking out above and below it by half the fist's height.
View: the fist seen from the front, the back of the fingers toward the viewer: four fingers curled round the rod in four horizontal stacked rows from top to bottom, each finger its own row of lit knuckles parted from the next by a dark line, the fingertips tucked round to the right side of the rod; the thumb lies across the top, wrapped over the first finger and over the rod; the heel of the palm is a shaded block down the right edge; the wrist is cut off flat just under the heel of the palm on the right. The fist is a compact block about four-fifths as wide as it is tall.
Pixel art: dark-fantasy pixel art like Image 1, every art pixel a hard square of one flat colour on one even grid, the fist about 26 art pixels wide and 32 tall, a 1-pixel dark outline (#231010), 3-4 flat tones per material, light from the top-left, no glow, no gradients, no dithering noise.
Constraints: exactly two fists and two rods, nothing else, no arm, no forearm, no bracer, no weapon head, no blade, no shadow, no floor, no labels, no text, on a flat pure magenta (#ff00ff) background.
