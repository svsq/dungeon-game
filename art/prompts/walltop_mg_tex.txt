Use case: a seamless tileable material for the top of the solid basalt mass at the magma cave's back wall, in a top-down 3/4 dungeon game (Image 1 is the style reference for pixel size and shading; Image 2 is the approved magma cave reference: take only the column tops and palette).
Subject: the flat tops of packed basalt columns seen from directly above: a mosaic of irregular hexagonal and pentagonal flat tops of mixed sizes (6 to 18 art pixels across), each with a one-pixel pale top-left bevel and a darker lower-right edge, near-black gaps between them, very dark neutral grey (#0e0d0e, #1c1a1b, #2a2727, bevels #3e3a39), low contrast.
Composition: the whole canvas is this surface, edge to edge, tiling seamlessly in both directions. Exactly 128x128 art pixels, every art pixel an 8x8 block of one colour.
Constraints: flat even lighting, no vignette, no gradient, no glow, no lava, no bricks, no blocks in a regular grid, no moss, no objects.
