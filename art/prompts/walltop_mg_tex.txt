Use case: a seamless tileable material for the top of the solid basalt mass at the magma cave's back wall, in a top-down 3/4 dungeon game (Image 1 is the style reference for pixel size and shading; Image 2 is a crop of the approved magma cave concept: take only the shape of its flat basalt column tops and its palette, not the lava).
Subject: the flat tops of tightly packed basalt columns seen from directly above: a mosaic of angular polygon tops with STRAIGHT edges and sharp corners, five or six sides each, 8 to 18 art pixels across, grouped in tight clusters of three or four; NOT round pebbles, NOT cobblestones, NOT river stones, NOT circles. Each top has a one-pixel pale bevel along its top and left edges and a darker lower-right edge, with near-black gaps between the columns. Very dark neutral grey (#0e0d0e, #1c1a1b, #2a2727, bevels #4a4544), low contrast.
Composition: the whole canvas is this surface, edge to edge, tiling seamlessly in both directions. Exactly 128x128 art pixels, every art pixel an 8x8 block of one colour.
Constraints: flat even lighting, no vignette, no gradient, no glow, no lava, no bricks, no blocks in a regular grid, no moss, no objects.
