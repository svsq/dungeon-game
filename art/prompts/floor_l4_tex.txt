Use case: a seamless tileable floor material for a top-down 3/4 dungeon game. Image 1 is the style reference: copy the stone floor near its lava cavern at the left (not the lava, not the walls).
Subject: a scorched volcanic flagstone floor seen straight from above: big worn basalt slabs of DIFFERENT sizes laid in an irregular pattern (some large squares, some long rectangles, some small filler stones, a few broken slabs split by cracks), chipped corners, uneven dark mortar, soot stains; dark charcoal and rust-brown stone with slight variation in tone from slab to slab, a few hairline cracks with dull dark-red rock inside (not glowing). Not a regular grid, not bathroom tiles.
Composition: the whole canvas is floor, edge to edge, tiling seamlessly in both directions. About 7 large slabs across the width. The canvas is 160x160 art pixels, every art pixel drawn as a block of one colour.
Constraints: flat even lighting, no vignette, no gradient across the image, no glow, no lava, no objects, no walls.
