Biome «Magma Caves» — a NATURAL VOLCANIC CAVE, not a built dungeon: this overrides the stone-block walls of the STYLE section. No masonry, no brick walls, no tiled floors: irregular organic cave walls of black basalt and dark red-brown volcanic rock with jagged edges, columns of basalt and stalagmites, still cut out of the pure black void and drawn with the same chunky pixel clusters and the same top-down 3/4 camera as Image 1. Floors: cracked dark basalt, ash, cinders and rubble, a few faintly glowing cracks. Lots of lava: a molten river winding through the caverns with dark cooled crust plates drifting on bright orange-yellow lava and white-hot edges, a lava lake with basalt islands, a lavafall pouring from a crack in the rock, small bubbling lava pools, steam vents. Crossings: a natural basalt arch over the river, a path of flat cooled stepping stones, an old chain-and-plank bridge. Light: lava is the main light — orange glow on the rock around it, strong falloff into black; a few warm torches on posts. Monsters: a big red horned demon, a green goblin, skeletons, a hooded cultist. A few bones, a charred chest, broken cages. The hero stands on a basalt ledge by the river fighting 2-3 monsters.
CAMERA (most important): exactly the same flat top-down 3/4 camera as Image 1 — the floor is seen almost straight from above and fills most of the frame; cave walls are LOW, drawn like the wall blocks of Image 1: only a short front face (about one tile high) along the edge of each cavern, never tall cliffs; no tall stalagmites or columns standing on the floor, at most a few short rocks; wide open walkable areas of floor where a fight can happen, not narrow ledges; the same muted, low-key palette and contrast as Image 1, saturated colour only in light sources and the water or lava. Lava covers under 30 % of the frame; only a few short basalt columns at cavern edges.
