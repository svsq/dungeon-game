Use case: a seamless tileable floor material for a top-down 3/4 dungeon game. Image 1 is the style reference: copy the stone floor of its blue arcane hall at the right (not the walls, no runes).
Subject: an old arcane hall floor seen straight from above: SMALL worn dark slate slabs of DIFFERENT sizes laid in an irregular pattern: most slabs about 12x12 art pixels, some long ones 24x12, some small 8x8 filler stones, a few slabs split by a crack, chipped corners, thin dark mortar, a little dust in the joints; cold blue-grey slate with slight variation in tone from slab to slab, a few slabs slightly smoother and darker. Not a regular grid, not bathroom tiles.
Composition: the whole canvas is floor, edge to edge, tiling seamlessly in both directions. About 12 slabs across the width and 12 down (a fine, dense paving, not big flagstones). The canvas is 160x160 art pixels, every art pixel drawn as a block of one colour.
Constraints: flat even lighting, no vignette, no gradient across the image, no glow, no runes, no symbols, no objects, no walls.
