Use case: a seamless tileable material for the tops of the thick ruined walls of the start town in a top-down 3/4 dungeon game. Image 1 is the style reference for pixel size, line and shading. Image 2 is the approved town concept: take only the tops of its ruined walls and palette.
Subject: the broken tops of dry-stone walls seen from directly above: uneven rubble of slate block tops in clearly different sizes, 6 to 20 art pixels across, placed with no grid and no straight joint running across the tile, overgrown in irregular patches with moss, grass tufts and a little ivy that hide most of the joints. Dark and low contrast: slate #1f181d, #2a2128, #372b34, moss #1f1c08, #302b09, #473e0b.
Composition: the whole canvas is this surface, edge to edge, tiling seamlessly in both directions. Exactly 128x128 art pixels, every art pixel an 8x8 block of one colour.
Constraints: flat even lighting, no vignette, no gradient, no flowers, no objects, no text.
