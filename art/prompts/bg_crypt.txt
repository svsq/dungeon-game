USE CASE: the painted backdrop behind the menus and end screens of the dark-fantasy top-down pixel-art dungeon crawler of Image 1. It is one opaque full-frame scene, landscape 16:9, edge to edge. Windows of parchment and leather are drawn over it later by the game, so most of it is seen only around their edges.
IMAGES: Image 1 is the style reference: match its pixel size, outlines, stone, palette and rendering, not its composition. Image 2 is the approved mock of the main menu: take only the idea of its backdrop scene (a ruined crypt gate with a round arch, a stair going down into a lit crypt corridor, two braziers on stone pedestals, two torn red banners, crumbling walls with moss and ivy, vignetted into black), never its interface, its parchment sheet, its hero or its text.
VIEW: the same camera as Image 1, a slightly raised front view of a wall and the floor before it, as the game's walls are seen; no fish-eye, no strong perspective, the arch faces the viewer.
SUBJECT AND COMPOSITION (positions as fractions of the frame, x from the left, y from the top):
- Centre, x 0.42-0.58: a massive round stone arch, its top at y 0.36, opening into a corridor that recedes into the crypt: ribbed vaults, two pairs of iron wall sconces holding dark unlit torch heads, a closed stone sarcophagus small at the far end, a few bones and a skull by the walls. A short wide stair of six to eight steps comes down from the arch toward the viewer and ends on a flat landing of worn flagstones at y 0.66-0.80, centred at x 0.47. The landing is empty and plain: a figure will be drawn standing on it.
- Either side of the arch, at x 0.38 and x 0.62, a square stone pedestal with an iron brazier bowl on it, the bowl rim at y 0.56. The bowls hold dark grey cold coals and nothing above them: no flame, no smoke, no sparks. Flames are drawn later by the game.
- On the wall above each brazier, at x 0.40 and x 0.60 from y 0.36 to 0.58, a hanging banner of deep red cloth (#73140d, #4f150c, highlights #af1f0c) with a torn ragged lower edge, plain, no emblem, no letters.
- Left third, x 0-0.34, and right third, x 0.66-1: dark ruined masonry, broken pillars, fallen blocks, a leaning gravestone, a little moss and ivy (#1a4135, #2b6c3d muted). Low detail and low contrast there: big quiet dark shapes, they will be covered by windows.
- Top third, y 0-0.32: the broken tops of the walls dissolve into black; an empty dark area with nothing in it (a title will be written there).
- Bottom: the floor flagstones continue to the bottom edge and fade into darkness at the corners. The top 8% and bottom 8% of the image will be cropped away: keep nothing important there.
LIGHT: the scene is lit as if both braziers and the corridor torches were burning: warm orange pools (#c25d1a, #f27f0e, highlights #eed68a) on the pedestals, the arch, the steps and the landing, fading to cold dark blue-grey stone (#3e3234, #252534, #141420) away from them, and to black (#060506) at the frame's edges and corners. Only the fires themselves are missing; draw no flame, no glow halo, no light rays, no bloom, no sparks or embers in the air. For this image the style bible's "no light sources lit" applies to the flames only; the warm light on the stone is wanted.
PIXEL ART: the whole frame is drawn as 960x540 art pixels: every art pixel is one hard square of one flat colour, the same size as an art pixel of Image 1's walls; 3-4 flat tone steps per material, dark mortar lines on the stone, no anti-aliasing, no blur, no smooth gradients (light falls off in flat steps or a 1-pixel dither), no noise.
PALETTE: stone #3e3234, #6b5c56, #9f9289 (lit edges only), #252534, #141420; outline and void #060506, #231010; warm light #c25d1a, #f27f0e, #eed68a; wood and leather #552f25, #733a21; red cloth #73140d, #4f150c, #af1f0c; moss #1a4135, #2b6c3d; bone #e3e2d7 in small touches.
