Biome «Arcane Halls» (floors 7-8): the blue hall of Image 1's right side, enlarged into a whole level. Cold blue-grey polished stone floors with engraved rune tiles, tall blue-flame braziers and blue crystal obelisks as the light sources (white-hot cores, blue pools of light), a circular stone fountain with glowing blue water in the middle of a great hall, bookshelves and stone statues, pillars. A long channel of luminous blue water runs along one hall with a small arched bridge. Monsters: a translucent blue ghost, armoured skeletons, a big red horned demon, a hooded cultist. A few warm candles as a second colour. Deep black void between halls.
