Use case: a UI panel texture for a pixel-art dungeon game. Image 1 is the style reference. Image 2 is the approved concept: copy the dark burnt-edged parchment sheet from it, the same deep tan and brown paper, burnt ragged edges, stains, and the four iron nails at its corners.
Subject: only the empty sheet of paper, about 1.6 times as wide as tall, filling most of the canvas, seen straight on. Completely blank: no text, no lines of writing, no portrait, no frame, no buttons, no icons, no seal.
The paper's middle must be even and calm, with low contrast, so dark text stays readable on it; keep stains and darker tones to the outer edges.
Hard square pixels, no gradients. Fully transparent outside the paper. No cast shadow, nothing else.
