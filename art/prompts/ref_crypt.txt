Biome «Crypts» (floors 3-4): the green crypt of Image 1's top right. Mossy dark stone floors with grave slabs, sickly toxic-green torch flames and green candles, a green glow pooling around a central sarcophagus. Rooms: a burial hall with stone coffins and gravestones in rows, an ossuary with skull niches and pillars, a ritual room with a glowing green circle. One room is partly flooded: dark still water covering half the floor with pale reflections of the green flames, stepping stones across it. Monsters: a hooded green cultist casting a green spark, a translucent blue ghost, skeletons. Cobwebs, bones, a closed chest, warm candle light only on one altar as a second colour.
